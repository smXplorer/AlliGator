AlliGator Panels
================

The main window of AlliGator is divided into two main areas: the 
**Source Image** panel to the left, and the different analysis panels to the 
right. Switching between panels is done by selecting the desired panel from the 
drop-down list at the top of the window on the right (below the menu bar).

Starting with version 1.09, these analysis panels can be detached (or undocked) 
from the main window by clicking on the blue pin object in the top left corner 
of the panel. The detached panel can then be resized as needed.

To dock a detached panel, simply click on the blue pin or close the panel.

The drop-down list of docked panel does not show detached panels.
